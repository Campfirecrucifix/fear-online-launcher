Welcome back to FEAR Online!

Play with your Steam friends - no servers, no port forwarding, and nobody sees anyone's IP (traffic goes through Steam's relay network).

 - HOST: starts a game server on your PC and gives you an invite code (copied to your clipboard).
 - JOIN: paste a friend's invite code and press JOIN - or right-click them in your Steam friends list > Join Game.

In game: log in with any name/password, pick a channel. The host makes a room (HOST GAME), friends double-click it in GAME ROOM LIST.

Found a bug? Press SUPPORT to report it.
